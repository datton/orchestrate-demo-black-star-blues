About

A fan site for people who grew up with two teams in their heart.

Who it is for

Black Star Blues is for Ghanaian Chelsea supporters living in England: children of the diaspora, new arrivals, and anyone who has cheered the Black Stars on Sunday and the Blues on Saturday. The colours here are red, gold and green, with the black star, and a good deal of blue.

Chelsea play at Stamford Bridge in Fulham, west London. Ghana became independent on 6 March 1957. Michael Essien, who played for Chelsea from 2005 to 2014, is one of the best-known links between the two.

What this site is not

  • Independent. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any football club or football association.
  • No club marks. It uses no club crest, logos or official photographs.
  • No live scores. For results and real fixtures, go to the official sources.
  • Samples are samples. Fixtures on this site are made-up examples, and are labelled that way.

Your information

The fan card and day planner run in your browser. Cards are saved in this browser’s local storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, there are no trackers, and you can remove a card at any time.

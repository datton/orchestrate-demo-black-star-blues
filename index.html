You can be loud for Accra and loyal to the Bridge. Nobody here asks you to pick one. This is a place for supporters who carry both stories to the ground, the pub or the sofa.

A match day, in four steps

Plan

  1. Plan

    Pick a match, set your journey time and work out when to leave. The day planner does the sums and shows them.

  2. Dress

    Blue for the club, red, gold and green for home. A scarf, a flag, or both.

  3. Gather

    Stadium, pub or living room: meet your people early, share the food, and settle in.

  4. Sing

    Ninety minutes of nerves and noise, then talk about it all the way home.
